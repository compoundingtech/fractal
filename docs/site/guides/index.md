# Guides

After your [first mission](../getting-started/first-mission.md), use these workflows as you need them.

- [Missions in practice](missions-in-practice.md): dependencies, revisions, queues, and review.
- [Talking to agents](talking-to-agents.md): durable conversation and explicit decisions.
- [Agent lifecycle](agent-lifecycle.md): restart, stop, suspend, and resume.
- [Two machines](two-machines.md) and [Fleet membership](fleet-join.md): connect your own machines.
- [GitHub integration](github-integration.md): observe repository work and route finite missions.
- [Terminal and iOS clients](terminal-and-ios.md) and [Build the iOS app](ios-app.md): follow and control work.
- [Scripted demos](demos.md): demonstrate a workflow without model usage.
- [Upgrading](upgrading.md) and [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): operate an existing installation safely.
